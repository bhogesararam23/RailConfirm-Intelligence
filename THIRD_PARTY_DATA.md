# Third-party data, services and attribution

The repository's Apache-2.0 licence applies to the original software and documentation it covers. It does not license third-party datasets, service output, trademarks, APIs or external content.

Before adding a source, record it in [the source register](docs/data/SOURCE_REGISTER.md) and verify the specific licence or contract.

For every external data source or provider, determine separately whether the planned use permits:
- retrieval and automated access;
- storage and retention;
- normalization and derived features;
- historical training-data creation;
- training and evaluation of a model;
- publication of aggregate evaluation results;
- redistribution of raw or transformed data;
- commercial deployment.

These permissions are not interchangeable. An API that permits displaying a response may not permit long-term storage or machine-learning use.

Do not put unlicensed datasets in the repository or distribute them as fixtures. Document any required attribution and preserve notices as required by the applicable terms.

No official railway endorsement or affiliation is implied by the project name or its use of publicly documented railway status terminology.
